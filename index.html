04 — Reflective Learning

Growing Through Reflection

Using Gibbs' Reflective Cycle to document meaningful learning experiences.

Description

Leading My First Agile Sprint

In my third-year project, I was unexpectedly asked to lead a two-week sprint for a team of five. We were building a clinic management system, and our previous sprint had missed its deliverables significantly.

Feelings

Anxious, then Motivated

Initially overwhelmed — I doubted my ability to organise others. But once I started breaking tasks down and communicating openly with the team, I felt increasingly capable and engaged.

Evaluation

What Worked and What Didn't

Daily standups improved team cohesion. However, I underestimated the time needed for testing, which created last-minute pressure. We delivered but not without stress.

Analysis & Action

Lessons Applied

I learned to buffer testing time in sprint planning. Going forward I adopted this discipline in all projects — it reduced our bug count by over 40% in subsequent sprints.
